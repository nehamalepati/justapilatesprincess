Liability Waiver, Release, and Assumption of Risk Agreement for classes and events hosted by Just A Pilates Princess LLC.

I knowingly and freely assume all such risks, both known and unknown, and assume full responsibility for my participation. I acknowledge that these risks cannot be eliminated regardless of the care taken by the Company.
